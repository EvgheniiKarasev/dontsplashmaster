01. About this policy

We are KAEV GAMES, SRL, an independent game developer based in the Republic of Moldova. This policy covers our games on the web, Google Play, the App Store, Steam, and Nintendo platforms that link to it, as well as related support.

Not every game uses every service. The information processed depends on the game, platform, and features you use. Stores and platforms also handle information under their own privacy policies.

02. Information & features

Our games and their service providers may process:

  • Technical information: IP address, device and browser details, operating system, language, app version, identifiers, and error reports.
  • Gameplay and advertising information: gameplay events, progress, settings, ad views and interactions, and advertising identifiers where available and permitted.
  • Multiplayer information: player identifiers or nicknames, connection details, and game-session data needed to connect players.
  • Support information: your email address and information you choose to send when contacting us.

This information helps deliver game features and ads, understand performance, fix problems, prevent abuse, and answer support requests. Where required by law, processing is based on consent, providing a requested service, legitimate interests such as security and support, or legal obligations.

Voice chat

Voice chat in Tralalero Arena and Rot Arena uses Photon Voice. With microphone permission, audio is transmitted to other participants. We do not record or save voice conversations. Photon may process technical connection data. You can mute your microphone or revoke access in device settings. Other players can hear you and may independently record a conversation.

Saves and downloads

Progress may be saved locally on your device. Some web games use CrazyGames cloud saves to store and restore progress through that platform.

Some web games download assets through Cloudflare R2 and Workers. Cloudflare processes technical request information, such as IP addresses and requested URLs, to deliver files and protect the service. Downloading game files is separate from cloud saves. Cloudflare also delivers this policy page and may process technical connection and performance information.

03. Services we use

  • Android / iOS: Google AdMob for ads, Firebase for analytics and related app services, and Photon in some multiplayer games.
  • Poki: Poki's advertising and analytics services, with Photon where multiplayer is supported.
  • CrazyGames: CrazyGames' advertising, analytics, and supported cloud-save services, with Photon where multiplayer is supported.
  • Steam: Steam's platform analytics and services.
  • Nintendo: Nintendo's platform services and analytics.
  • Web downloads: Cloudflare where used for game-file delivery.

These providers may process information on our behalf or independently under their own policies. They may operate in countries other than yours; applicable international-transfer safeguards must be used where required.

Learn more: Google / AdMob, Firebase, Photon, Poki, CrazyGames, Cloudflare, Steam, Nintendo, and Apple.

We may also use supporting technical providers for hosting, diagnostics, security, or support, limited to the purposes described here. Material changes to data use will be explained in this policy or a game-specific notice, with consent where required. We may disclose information when required by law.

04. Your choices

Games and platforms may use SDKs, cookies, or local storage for gameplay, analytics, and advertising. Use available game or platform consent controls, device advertising and tracking settings, and browser storage controls to manage your choices. Disabling personalised ads does not necessarily remove ads or all technical processing. Clearing local storage may erase local progress.

Advertising-related data sharing may count as targeted advertising, “sale”, or “sharing” under some laws. Where applicable, you may opt out through available privacy controls or contact us for help. Reading this policy or playing a game does not replace required consent.

Depending on your location, you may request access, correction, deletion, or a copy of your information, object to or restrict processing, withdraw consent, and complain to a data-protection authority. Email us with the game name, platform, and relevant player ID if available. We may need proportionate verification and will respond within the period required by law. For information controlled by a platform, such as its account or cloud saves, its own privacy process applies.

05. Children & families

Our games are not made specifically for children under 13, but some have content suitable for younger players. Please check the particular game's rating and features. A suitable age rating does not mean that ads, analytics, or voice chat automatically switch to a child mode.

Parents and guardians should review the game's online features and available platform controls. Any parental consent required by law must be obtained separately; this policy does not provide it. If you believe your child's information has been collected improperly, contact us. We will investigate and take the steps required by law, including deleting information under our control where appropriate.

06. Keeping information

We keep information under our control only as long as needed for the relevant feature, support, security, or legal obligations, then delete or anonymise it. For example, support emails may be kept while resolving a request and for necessary follow-up. We do not retain voice-chat recordings.

Local saves remain until removed through the game, browser, or device settings. Providers apply their own retention rules and service settings to analytics, ads, multiplayer records, and platform saves. Deleting local game data does not automatically delete a platform's cloud copy.

We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no online service is completely secure.
