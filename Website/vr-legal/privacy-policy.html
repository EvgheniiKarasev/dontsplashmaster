1. Who we are and what this covers

KAEV GAMES, SRL, Republic of Moldova (“we”, “us”), is responsible for the information we process as described here. This policy covers our VR games for Meta Quest that link to it, including Don't Get Wet!, related support, and these VR legal pages.

The information used depends on the features of each game. Any additional collection or online features will be explained in a game-specific notice before they are used. Our games on other platforms are covered by their linked policies.

2. Information we process and why

  • Game progress and settings. Games store progress, checkpoints, earned items, upgrades and preferences on your device so you can continue playing and retain your choices.
  • VR input. Headset and controller position, rotation, button input and tracking status are processed during play to render the scene, control the game and handle pauses. In Don't Get Wet!, movement data is not recorded or sent to us.
  • Local diagnostics. A game may keep a limited local log of technical events to help diagnose problems. Don't Get Wet! keeps a short, rotating log of game, pause and tracking states. It is not automatically uploaded; you may choose to send it to support.
  • Support requests. If you contact us, we receive your email address and the message, screenshots or diagnostic files you provide. We use them to answer you and investigate the issue. Do not send passwords or payment-card details.
  • Visits to these pages. Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, processes technical request information such as IP address, browser information, requested URL and request timing to deliver and secure the pages and diagnose errors. These pages do not add advertising trackers or analytics scripts.

Don't Get Wet! is an offline, single-player game. It has no in-game account, advertising, developer analytics, chat or cloud-save service. It does not send gameplay data to us or request microphone, camera, contacts or precise-location access. Its use of headset tracking for gameplay is distinct from recording camera footage.

Where a legal basis is required, we process information to provide the requested game or support, for legitimate interests in service security and troubleshooting, to meet legal obligations, or with consent where required. This policy is not a substitute for any required consent.

3. Service providers and Meta

We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising in the games covered by this policy. Hosting and email providers may process information needed to deliver these pages or handle support on our behalf. We may disclose information where required by law or where legally necessary to protect rights and security.

Meta independently handles its platform account, store purchases, headset services and any system recording or sharing features you choose to use. These activities are governed by Meta's Privacy Policy and your platform settings. We do not receive your payment-card details from the store.

Cloudflare's processing is described in its Privacy Policy. Service providers may process information in other countries; applicable safeguards are used where international-transfer rules require them.

4. How long information is kept

Local progress and settings remain until you clear the game's app data or uninstall it. Local diagnostic logs are limited and replaced over time. Copies you export or send elsewhere must be managed separately.

We keep support correspondence and attachments only as long as needed to resolve the request, provide necessary follow-up, and meet applicable legal or security obligations, then delete or anonymise them. Hosting and email providers apply their service retention settings. We take reasonable steps to protect information under our control.

5. Delete your data or make a privacy request

All users, regardless of country, can request deletion free of charge. Email support@kaevgames.com with the subject “VR privacy request”, the game title, and a description of what you want deleted. For support correspondence, use the email address you contacted us from when possible. We may ask for proportionate information to verify the request; do not send passwords.

To remove data stored only on your headset, clear the game's app data using the available device controls or uninstall the game. We cannot remotely erase offline saves. In Don't Get Wet!, NEW RUN resets the run but keeps permanent upgrades; it is not a full data-deletion option. Delete any diagnostic copies you exported separately.

We will handle requests within the time required by applicable law. If limited information must be retained to meet a legal obligation or establish, exercise or defend legal claims, we will explain the reason and retain only what is necessary. For Meta account, purchase or other information controlled by Meta, use Meta's privacy controls or contact Meta.

Depending on applicable law, you may also request access, correction or a copy of your information, object to or restrict processing, withdraw consent, and complain to a data-protection authority. Contact us at the same address to exercise these rights.

6. Age requirements and changes

Follow the game's stated age rating, intended audience and the platform's age requirements. Don't Get Wet! is intended for teens and adults aged 13 or older, or the applicable minimum age in your region. If you believe a child has improperly provided personal information to us, contact us so we can investigate and delete it where appropriate.

We will update this policy when our practices change and provide additional notice or obtain consent where required. The date above identifies this version.
